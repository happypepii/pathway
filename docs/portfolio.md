## 3. Engineering Achievements

Only add verified achievements to this section.

For each achievement, record:

**Area:** Backend / Infrastructure / Product

**Problem:** What was the original issue?

**Impact:** Why did the problem matter?

**My Contribution:** What did I personally investigate, design, implement, or verify?

**Solution:** What was changed, and why?

**Alternatives:** What other approaches were considered?

**Verification:** What tests, measurements, or experiments support the result?
